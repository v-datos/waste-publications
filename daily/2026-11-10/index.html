Ottawa asks residents how to manage the garbage left after diversion

Ottawa opened its long-term waste management consultation on November 9 and is taking survey responses through December 14. The city says it has secured additional landfill capacity and is also studying waste-to-energy incineration for garbage remaining after reduction, reuse, recycling and composting. Incineration is still an option under study. The first open house is at the Trail Waste Facility Landfill on November 26. City of Ottawa consultation.

A useful question for the next technical study is whether both disposal options are sized against the same residual-tonnage scenarios. Running both options against the same assumptions about organics and recycling capture would make their capacity requirements easier to compare.

Sundre considers lower residential recycling charges

For Sundre's November 9 council meeting, staff recommended second and third readings of Bylaw 2026-06 to pass savings from revised residential recycling costs through to monthly utility bills. The council vote and first affected bill were still to be confirmed. Circular Materials lists October 1 as the start of its management of Sundre's recycling program. Sundre agenda, item 8.2, Circular Materials service page.

For a municipal budget comparison, record the service transfer date separately from the date a revised charge appears on a bill. That lets finance staff reconcile the change in who runs the service with the change households see.

Research spotlight — check forecast errors by type of day

A November 7 preprint compared four models for daily municipal waste in one Sri Lankan municipality. The authors trained on 2012–2017 data and tested on 2018. The long short-term memory model had the lowest overall mean absolute percentage error at 4.62%. In a separate analysis that trained weekday and weekend models on separate subsets, only SARIMAX reproduced the higher average waste on weekdays. The study used one municipality and one test year. The authors interpolated missing dates and replaced flagged outliers with values from a week earlier. Dey and colleagues, sections 4.2–4.4 and 5.2, and Table 3.

Worth acting on For a Banff/Canmore forecasting trial, compare candidate models with a same-weekday baseline. Score ordinary days and holiday-shifted collections separately, and flag dates with no scheduled collection separately from missing weigh tickets.
