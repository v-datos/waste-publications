Canmore budgets for higher contracted waste service costs

The November 10 Finance Committee agenda put 2027 solid waste services spending at $6.10 million in the draft budget, up from $5.80 million budgeted for 2026. The contracted services line rises from $1,223,058 to $1,305,650. Staff list higher collected food waste volumes as one of several factors behind that increase. The business plan also calls for continuing to expand the residential food waste program. Town of Canmore Finance Committee agenda, item D1 and PDF pages 52–53.

For the budget discussion, ask for the expected food waste tonnes by customer sector and the collection, haul and processing charges tied to those tonnes. Tracking tonnes alongside unit rates and fixed charges would help explain later variances, including whether spending moved because more material came in or because the service cost more to provide.

Ontario posts air and noise approval for a Leamington organics facility

The Environmental Registry of Ontario posted its decision on November 10. The approval covers air and noise for Seacliff Renewables’ organic waste recycling and bioenergy facility. The decision lists the biofilter, cogeneration unit, biogas upgrading equipment, flare and boiler among the emission sources. The original application describes upgrading biogas for injection into the natural gas distribution network. Environmental Registry of Ontario decision 025-1416, original proposal.

For a municipal organics procurement, the notice is a reminder to ask bidders which equipment controls odour and how the operator will document its performance. Request operating records for those controls when comparing facilities.

Research spotlight — give paper subcategories their own rows

Hawaiʻi’s Department of Health used two rounds of hand sorting at four facilities to estimate the composition of disposed residential and commercial municipal solid waste. In the September 29 final report, paper accounted for an estimated 24.59% by weight. Uncoated corrugated cardboard accounted for 9.22% of the whole stream, and paper the study classified as potentially compostable accounted for 7.33%. The study assessed a separate construction and demolition stream visually and reported those results on their own. Hawaiʻi Statewide Waste Characterization Study, Table 2-1 and Table 5-2.

Worth acting on For the next Bow Valley sort, record clean corrugated cardboard and food-soiled paper separately. Before assigning either category to a diversion estimate, match the sort definitions to what the local recycler and organics processor accept.
