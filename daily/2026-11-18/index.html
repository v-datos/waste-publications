Lethbridge keeps Saturday access but ends free disposal

Lethbridge announced November 17 that its Waste & Recycling Centre will apply regular disposal rates on Saturdays starting January 1, 2027. Saturday hours stay at 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. The city puts the operating budget savings at $258,750. It also points residents to year-round recycling drop-off stations and a separate large-item service. City of Lethbridge.

For a similar fee change in the Bow Valley, compare Saturday and weekday visits as well as tonnes against the same weeks in the previous year. If Saturday visits fall but weekday visits rise, some trips may have shifted rather than disappeared. Check what happened to total tonnage before drawing a conclusion about disposal.

Alberta asks operators about waste facility practices

In a provincial invitation published November 17 by the Recycling Council of Alberta, Agriculture and Irrigation seeks input on rat prevention, detection and response. Environment and Protected Areas is using the same survey to learn about operations, environmental issues and nuisances at recycling facilities and storage sites. The stakeholder survey closes December 6. It's a request for input on possible changes. Provincial invitation via RCA · Alberta engagement page.

A carton blend shows promise as insulation, with a measurable tradeoff

On November 17 the Carton Council of Canada shared a pilot by Innofibre and SEREX, dated October 12. The team removed caps and straws from recovered cartons before grinding and refining them. At a fixed test density of 50 kg/m³, the blend with 25% cartons had thermal conductivity of 0.0449 W/(m·K), versus 0.0407 for the cellulose comparator. Higher conductivity means less thermal resistance at the same thickness. Announcement · Pilot report, section 2 and Table 4.

The two flame-retardant-treated carton samples passed a cigarette ignition test. The report says that result alone does not establish compliance with the Canadian loose-fill insulation standard. This is a possible outlet for recovered cartons; the pilot does not establish commercial demand for bales.

Chatham-Kent seeks more time to review the Dresden proposals

On November 17, Chatham-Kent said it asked Ontario to extend public comments on York1's proposed Dresden landfill and waste transfer and processing facility to February 4, 2027. The municipality wants time to finish technical reviews and hear from residents and Indigenous communities. Ontario's landfill notice still lists December 21, 2026; the municipality says it is awaiting a response. Chatham-Kent update · Ontario landfill notice 026-0992.
