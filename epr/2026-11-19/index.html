Regulatory developments

Alberta · ONGOING consultation on 2027 oversight fees

ARMA's consultation began October 27 and closes December 11 at 11:59 p.m. ABT, as posted. The draft individual producer oversight fees are:

Stream Draft flat fee Draft variable rate
Single-use products, packaging and paper products (PPP) CAD 180 CAD 0.0307/kg
Hazardous and special products (HSP) CAD 325 CAD 0.0733/kg

ARMA lists final 2026 variable rates of CAD 0.0334/kg for PPP and CAD 0.1026/kg for HSP. These are regulator oversight charges; they do not establish a municipality's collection contract price or a PRO's total service charge. Producers should check the applicable supply threshold and fee calculation before forecasting liabilities. The 2027 amounts remain draft. Municipal budget discussions should distinguish these charges from any local service savings. ARMA, oversight fees and consultation

Ontario · UPDATED legislative stage; ONGOING policy options

The Assembly's status table records November 17 consideration of Bill 140 by the Standing Committee on the Interior. Its agenda included municipal and waste-sector representatives. The written-submission deadline was 6 p.m. EST that day. The inspected record shows committee consideration, without Royal Assent. Bill status · Committee agenda · Submission notice

The companion Environmental Registry proposal remains open until November 26 at 11:59 p.m., as posted. Options include regulating contracts and fees, changing flexible-plastic obligations and allowing material from small industrial, commercial and institutional (IC&I) establishments to count toward producer targets. The ministry describes possible implementation in 2027, conditional on legislative passage and subsequent regulation. For municipal submissions, identify the service and cost implications of each option separately. Counting IC&I tonnes toward a target would not, by itself, establish universal business collection entitlement. Environmental Registry proposal 026-1058

Ontario · UPDATED RPRA consultation has closed

RPRA now places its Blue Box audit and supply-reporting consultation among past consultations. It ran October 2–November 16, and the regulator says it is reviewing feedback. The drafts address verification of management performance reports and annual supply reporting; finalized documents are intended for the 2027 reporting cycle. The consultation page does not establish that final documents have been issued. Producers, PROs and their auditors should watch for final publication before treating draft instructions as settled. For municipal processors supplying records, preserve the distinction between material supplied to market and weights supporting recovered-resource reporting. RPRA consultation and next steps

British Columbia · UPDATED battery-plan calendar

Call2Recycle scheduled amended-plan webinars for November 12, 16 and 18 within this reporting window, following the first session on November 10. The operator's page establishes the schedule, not attendance or consultation conclusions. Written comments on the proposed addition of vapes/e-cigarettes, 12-volt batteries and portable power remain due December 8 at 5 p.m. PCT, as posted. This remains a plan consultation. Municipal receiving sites can use comments to seek specific collection, staffing, equipment and payment arrangements for proposed new streams before changing local acceptance instructions. Call2Recycle consultation

Program and municipal operations

Alberta · NEW Lethbridge notice for a separate battery collection

The city's November 16 announcement sets out collection between November 17 and 27 on residents' scheduled green-cart day. It accepts household single-use AA, AAA, C, D and 9V batteries in sealed, resealable plastic bags placed on top of the green cart. Residents without a curbside green cart can take the bags to the Waste & Recycling Centre during business hours. This is a separate battery collection arrangement: follow the stated placement instruction and accepted battery types when sharing it with residents. The notice does not report collected weight or recycling output. City of Lethbridge instructions

Saskatchewan · ONGOING two different Phase 2 transition paths

SK Recycles continues to list December 1, 2026 as the final planned community-led Phase 2 launch date. For program-led service, it lists provider selection and announcement as expected in November 2026, with service commencement on February 1, 2028. The inspected timetable names no successful provider. Community-led collectors retain responsibility for collection and education, while SK Recycles handles post-collection services; under the program-led model, the operator takes responsibility for both. Municipal staff should match resident notices and contract planning to their actual model and obtain confirmation of local arrangements before announcing a handover. A selection timetable does not establish an award or a completed transition. SK Recycles Phase 2 timetable and model descriptions

Ontario · UPDATED scheduled northern collection milestones

Manitouwadge: a November 6 township notice sets November 16 as the start of Circular Materials management of the recycling program, with GFL continuing collection and an updated accepted-material list. It tells residents to continue curbside set-out. The management change should therefore be communicated alongside the material guide; it does not imply a new hauler. The notice establishes the scheduled transition, without confirming completed collection rounds. Township notice

Hornepayne: the township's October 16 notice schedules collection every other Tuesday beginning November 17, using the new recycling bin. This week's milestone is the advertised start date, not a newly published announcement. Municipal communications can direct residents to the community-specific calendar and material instructions rather than treating a province-wide transition date as a local pickup schedule. No first-round collection result is given in this notice. Township notice

Quebec · NEW openings in Montréal and Laval

AQRCB/Consignaction's November 12 release announces a return site at 2441 rue Notre-Dame Ouest, in Montréal's Sud-Ouest. It offers single-container returns and express bag drop-off. Express refunds are electronic after sorting and counting. Operator release

The November 16 release announces a Consignaction+ site at 1000 desserte Chomedey Ouest, in Sainte-Dorothée, Laval. Operator release

These are deposit-bearing beverage-container locations. Municipal service directories can add the advertised sites while retaining the distinction between deposit returns and other packaging collection. Both sources are operator announcements; neither provides measured site throughput or evidence of a change in province-wide recovery performance.

Ontario · NEW North Bay diversion-site interruption. North Bay's November 17 advisory closed public access to its Waste Diversion Facility at 112 Patton Street for November 18 after a fire in a building on the site. The affected services included household hazardous waste, electronics, scrap metal, leaf and yard waste, and residential recycling drop-off. The city said it was working toward reopening later in the week. This advisory does not confirm reopening or identify the fire's cause. Service desks should obtain a subsequent city confirmation before directing visitors back to the site. City advisory

Infrastructure, end markets and financial signals

Alberta · UPDATED Lethbridge disposal pricing notice

The November 17 city notice says standard rates will apply to all loads at the Waste & Recycling Centre, including Saturdays, from January 1, 2027. It implements the discontinuation of free Saturday disposal approved in the earlier 2027 operating budget. The city attributes CAD 258,750 in operating-budget savings to the change. This is the city's financial estimate; the notice gives no measured change in disposal tonnage, illegal dumping or diversion. For municipal comparisons, this bulletin recommends keeping fee revenue and budget savings separate from environmental performance. Update local referral information before the effective date. City pricing notice

The return-site openings and collection notices above establish advertised access and service arrangements. They do not support a new commissioned processing-capacity estimate or an end-market sales forecast. When evaluating service bids, request receiving-facility and destination information for each material, and distinguish tonnes collected from verified recycling, inventory and disposal.

What to watch next

  • Alberta and Western municipal implementation: confirm local collection arrangements and material instructions before changing resident guidance. Watch for Saskatchewan provider awards and confirmation of community-led launches. Keep the BC battery-plan additions at consultation status until their approval and implementation terms are established.
  • Ontario decisions: watch for the next Bill 140 legislative step, the government's response after the policy-comment period, and RPRA's final audit and reporting documents. Each has a separate process; none of the inspected draft proposals establishes a present change to local collection entitlement.
  • Operational evidence: seek confirmation of North Bay reopening and measured results from newly advertised collection access. For future performance comparisons, require a defined period, material scope and denominator, and distinguish residential service from IC&I arrangements.

This edition uses selected government, regulator, municipal and program-operator records.
